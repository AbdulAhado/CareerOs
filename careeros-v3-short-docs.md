# CareerOS AI v3 — Executive Overview

CareerOS AI v3 is an elite, interconnected **AI Ecosystem** designed to provide recruiter-grade career guidance. It replaces simple chatbots with a multi-layered, stateful reasoning engine.

## Core Differentiators

1. **The Ecosystem Memory (`UserIntelligenceProfile`)**
   The AI maintains a continuous memory of the user. If the ATS Analyzer flags a weak resume, the Career Coach automatically knows and adjusts its roadmap. It tracks target salaries, skill gaps, visa requirements, and interview readiness across the entire platform.

2. **The 15-Expert Council**
   The AI doesn't act as a generic assistant. It combines the perspectives of 15 industry experts—including a FAANG Recruiter, a Startup Founder, and a Compensation Specialist—enforced by a strict "AI Quality Layer" that eliminates fluff and generic advice.

3. **Multi-Pass Reasoning (Chain of Thought)**
   Before answering, the AI internally documents its *Intent Detection*, *Context Analysis*, and *Revision Plan*. This eliminates hallucinations and ensures deeply personalized outputs.

4. **Transparent Confidence Scoring**
   Every AI output includes an explicit confidence score (e.g., 85%) and a detailed explanation of *why* it gave that score, establishing high user trust.

## Feature Highlights

- **Career Coach:** Generates dynamic, step-by-step career roadmaps instead of one-off answers.
- **ATS Analyzer:** Performs true algorithm simulation, detecting synonym matching, domain alignment, and logistical filters (Visa/Clearance).
- **Resume Analyzer:** Audits resumes at an executive level, scoring "Business Impact", "Ownership", and "Executive Presence".
- **Proposal Generator:** Acts as a client psychologist, detecting hidden urgency and adapting tone before writing the proposal.
- **Adaptive Interviews:** Dynamically scales question difficulty up or down based on the candidate's previous answer scores.

*CareerOS v3 is not just an AI wrapper; it is an intelligent, unified career strategy platform.*
